About me

I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Amazon's Annapurna Labs, specializing in optimizing large language model inference performance on AWS AI accelerators — including Inferentia and Trainium. I work on designing developer-friendly APIs for the AWS Neuron software stack, with deep expertise in inference optimization techniques such as KV Caching, Automatic Prefix Caching, and parallelism across tensor, data, and sequence dimensions. I'm passionate about pushing the boundaries of LLM performance at production scale.

I am currently part of the AWS Neuron team, building a high-performance distributed inference and training library designed to accelerate deep learning workloads through parallelism across tensor, data, and sequence dimensions. I onboarded Llama 4 and Qwen 3 into the Neuron library, integrating custom NKI kernels and enabling key inference features including Automatic Prefix Caching, KV Caching, and auto-bucketing. Most recently, I optimized Qwen 3 inference on Neuron for a 3× improvement in latency, driving gains in TTFT, throughput, and cost per million tokens at production scale.

Earlier at Amazon, I worked on Prime Video's recommendation systems — designing a Bayesian neural bandit algorithm for personalized content recommendations, building a two-tower deep neural network for propensity scoring, and fine-tuning a BERT-based ranking model with triplet loss. I owned the system end to end, from development through deployment and A/B testing, and the resulting recommendation deployment drove a 35% increase in customer engagement across a sample of 200 million customers worldwide.

Before Amazon, I built search and data infrastructure at Oracle, Ticketmaster, and Cognizant — including a BERT embedding-based model that lifted search Precision@10 by 18 points, and scalable data pipelines for enterprise data warehousing.

I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science & Statistics from Arizona State University, where I graduated with a 4.00 GPA.
